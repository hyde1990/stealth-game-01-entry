Midnight Gallery — a top-down stealth game: a cat burglar slips into a gallery after dark to steal three moon-gems and slip out through the skylight exit unseen. Four guards patrol the halls with vision cones clipped by walls and display cases; crouch behind cover, hide in the potted plants, and never run where they can hear you. Suspicion builds while a guard sees or hears you — at full suspicion the guard gives chase, and being touched means getting caught. Break line of sight to cool the alarm down.

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD / arrow keys, or click the floor to walk there
- **Sneak:** hold Shift — slower, silent, harder to spot
- **Pause:** P or Esc · **Mute:** M · **Restart:** R
- **Goal:** steal all 3 gems, then reach the green EXIT. Faster escapes score more.

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser — no network needed, everything runs offline. Sounds are synthesized in the browser; there are no audio files.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```

Then open `output/index.html` in a browser — or serve `game/` directly and open `game/index.html`.
